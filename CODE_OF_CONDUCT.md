# Code of conduct

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to learn something, because in
this repository they almost certainly are.

## In practice

- Critique the code, the number or the sentence, not the person who wrote it.
- "This is wrong, and here is the measurement" is welcome and wanted. "This is obvious" is not.
- Hebrew or English, whichever you think in. Neither is corrected for style in an issue.
- Beginners' questions are the point of the project, not an interruption to it.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or publishing anyone's private
information.

## Enforcement

Report anything to itay.cohen2907@gmail.com. Reports stay private. Comments may be edited or
removed and accounts may be blocked; a decision will be explained to the person affected.
