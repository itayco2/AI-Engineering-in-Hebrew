# Where this data came from

`meridian.json` was written for this chapter. `Meridian` is not a real product and the
documentation describes nothing that exists.

That is deliberate, and it is the reason the chapter can make claims. Every document was
written to contain a specific phenomenon worth measuring: error codes that only exact
matching finds, pairs of documents whose subjects are near-identical without their titles,
and questions phrased so they share almost no words with their answers. A scraped corpus
would be more realistic and would make every number in the chapter unfalsifiable.

- **46 documents**, 246-469 characters each
- **28 questions**, each with the documents that genuinely answer it
- Questions are labelled `identifier`, `paraphrase` or `mixed`, which is what lets the
  chapter show that different retrieval mechanisms fix different failures
